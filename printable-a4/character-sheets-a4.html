Quick reference cards

Roll rule

Roll d6 + stat stars. 1–2 wobble. 3–4 yes, but. 5+ bright success.

Stats

Brave faces pressure. Kind helps feelings. Quick moves, spots, and sneaks.

Turn prompt

“What do you try?” then “How does your hero do it?”

Kindness rule

Heroes can be muddy, surprised, or silly. The story never shames a child for helping.

Wobble ideas

Lost hat, wet map, wrong door, sleepy troll, ringing bell, muddied boots.

End of session

Name one friend made, one brave choice, and one thing to draw.
