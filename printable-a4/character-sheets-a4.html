Quick reference cards

Roll rule

Roll d6 + stat bonus. 1–3 wobble. 4–5 yes, but. 6+ bright success.

HP / MP

HP handles bumps and tiredness. MP fuels spells. Cantrips cost 0 MP.

Stats

Strength lifts/protects. Int solves/knows. Agility moves/sneaks. Wis notices/understands. Luck handles charms/surprises.

Turn prompt

“What do you try?” then “How does your hero do it?”

Kindness rule

Heroes can be muddy, surprised, or silly. The story never shames an Adventurer for helping.

Wobble ideas

Lost hat, wet map, wrong door, sleepy troll, ringing bell, muddied boots.

End of session

Name one friend made, one brave choice, and one thing to draw.
