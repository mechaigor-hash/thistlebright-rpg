How to run these adventures

Session recipe

  • Read the boxed opening.
  • Ask what the adventurers notice.
  • Run two scenes and one creature moment.
  • Give treasure that changes the next scene.
  • End with a friend, promise, or map mark.
Length: each adventure is built for 30–45 minutes.

DM checklist

  • Circle one feeling: scared, proud, lonely, sleepy, worried.
  • Underline one clue the heroes can touch.
  • Pick one NPC voice: whispery, grand, grumpy, giggly.
  • Choose one safe cost before rolling: noise, mud, delay, lost hat.

Scaling

GroupChange
1 adventurerReduce enemy HP by 4 and MP by 2.
2–3 adventurersUse listed stats.
4+ adventurersAdd one clue objective or +3 HP to the main creature.
Older AdventurersAdd a timer, locked door, or rival helper.

1. The Bell-Root Path

Read aloud: Tiny bells hang from sleepy roots. A fox dreams beside a moon-rune door, and the path is holding its breath.

Scenes

  • Notice the bells before they ring.
  • Find the silver acorn key.
  • Wake the fox kindly or sneak past.

Main creature

Redcap Bramble Sneak, DR 3, wants its shiny bell back.

Checks

ApproachStatWobble
Careful movementAgilityNoise, snag, or muddy boot.
Read signs/runesIntRight answer, awkward timing.
Comfort or negotiateWisCreature asks for proof.
Hold, lift, protectStrengthYou help, but lose 1 HP.
Fairy chanceLuckMagic works with a funny mark.

Treasure

Silver acorn key; 6 copper; fox promise.

Read-aloud closer

Ask each adventurer what their hero looks like at the end of the scene: muddy boots, proud smile, glowing pocket, or new friend.

If they get stuck

Show one clue in the art, have an NPC ask a simple question, or let a pet notice the safest path.

Optional twist

A rival helper arrives, the weather changes, or the treasure points to the next map mark.

Level note

After a big kindness, mark one star toward the next level.
