How to use creature stat blocks

Creatures in Adventures in Alba are more than numbers, but the numbers help the Guide pace scenes. Every creature has HP, MP, Level, a Difficulty Rating, classic stats, and plenty of story hooks.

Creature rule: every creature has a feeling, a wish, useful stats, and a safe complication.
Creature rolls: when a creature acts against a hero, roll d6 + the creature stat. Use results to create funny trouble, not punishment.
StatCreature use
StrengthPush, hold, carry, stomp, protect.
IntRiddles, tricks, memories, old magic.
AgilityRace, dodge, fly, swim, sneak.
WisFeelings, nature, noticing, animal sense.
LuckFairy weirdness, charm magic, surprises.

Difficulty Rating

Formula: DR = round((HP + MP + Level×3 + positive stat bonuses×2) ÷ 10). Minimum DR is 1.
DRMeaningUse at table
1Tiny troubleOne child can usually solve it with a good idea.
2Small sceneGood for a warm-up creature or comic obstacle.
3Fair challengeNeeds teamwork, a spell, or two rolls.
4Big sceneUse as the main creature for an adventure.
5+Boss or wonderUse rarely; make it emotional, not scary.
Balancing tip: for 5–7 year olds, “harder” should mean more choices and more wonder, not harsher consequences.

Moon-Kelpie Foal

A nervous water-pony child with a kelp mane and a moon-bell collar. It wants to be brave but splashes when startled.

It circles moonlit shallows where lost songs sink. If heroes speak gently, it lowers its head so the bell-shaped mark on its collar can be seen. It is not trying to block the path; it is afraid the loch will forget bedtime.

Example: A kelp-mane pony has HP 9, MP 4, Level 1, Strength +1, Agility +2, Wis +1, Luck +1. DR = round((9+4+3+10)/10) = 3.

Set HP and MP

Tiny: HP 6–8, MP 2–4. Normal: HP 9–12, MP 3–6. Big: HP 15+, MP 2–8.

Set the stats

Pick one +3, one +2, two +1, and one +0. Very tiny creatures may have one -1 and one +3.

Calculate DR

DR = round((HP + MP + Level×3 + positive stats×2) ÷ 10). Minimum 1.

Make it child-friendly

Replace defeat with help, chase, song, puzzle, promise, snack, or finding a lost thing.

Creature tactics without scary combat

What enemies do

  • Guard: blocks a path until a promise is made.
  • Grab: takes a hat, map, boot, spoon, or bell.
  • Hide: runs into mist, reeds, cupboards, or clouds.
  • Challenge: asks for a race, riddle, song, or brave sentence.
  • Spend MP: uses one magical move, then gets tired or silly.
At 0 HP: enemies stop, slump, nap, cry, apologise, or ask for help. No gore, no cruelty.
