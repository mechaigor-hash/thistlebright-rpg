Gear scenes

  • Rope makes teamwork visible: one climbs, one holds, one watches.
  • Lanterns should show clues, not just remove darkness.
  • Tools let small hands fix things instead of smash them.
  • Food is social magic: it starts conversations.
Rule: gear gives +1 only when the player describes how it helps.

Magic items

Rowan-Bark Shield

Reduces thorn, arrow, or fear trouble by 2 HP. Wants to protect someone smaller.

Loch-Mirror Cloak

+1 to sneak in mist or moonlight. Shows the wearer one honest reflection.

Cairn-Knuckle Ring

+1 to hold, lift, or remember an oath. Heavy when lies are near.

Star-Moth Lantern

Reveals invisible ink, ghost tracks, and shy fairies if spoken to politely.

Mythral Seed Blade

Cuts curses and refuses cruelty. It becomes dull if used to bully.

Bridge-Friend Badge

Roads and bridges answer one practical question per adventure.
